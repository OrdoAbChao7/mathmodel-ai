# Mechanistic and Physical Models

Start from conservation laws, balance equations, causal mechanisms, or domain constraints rather than selecting an algorithm by name. Declare every variable's unit, sign convention, admissible range, initial/boundary condition, and parameter source. Distinguish calibrated parameters from independently measured quantities and state the identifiability limits.

Check Dimensional consistency, boundary behavior, parameter plausibility, calibration residuals, and comparison with a simpler or external reference model. Attack the mechanism with limiting cases and parameter perturbations. A good fit alone does not establish that the mechanism is correct.

In the paper, derive the governing equations and assumptions before numerical details, show a unit table and boundary-case result in the body, and tie each physical conclusion to a result and validation ID.

# Quality Checklist

- Problem data and units are traceable.
- Every assumption is explicit and defensible.
- Symbols, dimensions, signs, and boundary conditions are consistent.
- Code reruns from a documented environment and seed.
- Tables and figures are generated from current results.
- Baseline, residual/error, sensitivity, and limitation analyses are present where applicable.
- No unresolved citations or cross-references; no overfull boxes or clipped content.
- PDF page numbering, margins, fonts, captions, bibliography, and appendix follow the latest official notice.

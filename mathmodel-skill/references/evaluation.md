# Evaluation Route

Define alternatives, indicators, units, desired direction, missing-data policy, and decision context. Normalize indicators with a method consistent with their direction and range; record the transformation so a reader can reproduce every score.

Justify weights through data, policy, expert elicitation, or a stated hybrid rule. Compute the ranking with a transparent method such as weighted scoring, TOPSIS, AHP, entropy weighting, or a justified combination. Compare at least one plausible weighting or normalization alternative when rankings drive recommendations.

Record normalized values, weights, method parameters, scores, ranks, and sensitivity results in the result and validation registries. Reject a definitive ranking when small justified weight changes reverse the order; report stable groups, uncertainty, or conditional recommendations instead.

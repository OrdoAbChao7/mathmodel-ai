\section*{Abstract}
This deterministic integration fixture solves a constrained allocation problem with a baseline and three alternative routes.
\section{Problem}
The objective is to minimize allocation cost subject to demand and capacity constraints.
\section{Assumptions and models}
The capacity and demand are measured in consistent units. The selected route is compared with a baseline.
\section{Validation and results}
The solver status, feasibility, objective recomputation, baseline comparison, and falsification records are machine checked.

The result is reproducible within the stated fixture scope. \cite{fixture-source}
